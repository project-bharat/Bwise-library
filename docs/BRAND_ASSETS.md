# Brand assets and build usage

The supplied kit is preserved in [`resources/brand/Bwise_Brand_Assets`](../resources/brand/Bwise_Brand_Assets). It contains 98 files (about 8.5 MB), including the original usage guide at `GUIDELINES.md`.

## Assets included

- **Google Play and Android:** `android/play_store_icon_512.png` is the full-bleed 512×512 Play listing icon. `android/res/` contains the adaptive icon background/foreground/monochrome layers and density-specific launcher icons. `android/res/drawable-nodpi/splash_icon_white.png` is the native splash mark.
- **Reader milestones:** `badges/` contains earned and locked artwork for 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, and 500 finished books, plus `badges_preview.png`.
- **Logos:** `logos/` provides horizontal and stacked color, mono, and white versions; `logos/tight/` contains tight mark variants.
- **Web:** `web/` provides favicon sizes, Apple touch icon, 192/512 PWA icons, a maskable icon, and transparent marks.
- **Social and store promotion:** `social/` includes the 1024×500 Play feature graphic, Open Graph images, social avatars, banners, square images, and a story image.
- **Patterns and references:** `patterns/` contains rack/vertical tile patterns and previews; the root includes construction, measurement, alignment, and Android mask previews.

The kit does **not** include Play listing screenshots or a promotional video. The badge illustrations contain English wording; the app localizes each badge's title and earned/locked status around the supplied art.

## Build integration

`python scripts/generate-icons.py --brand` copies the official transparent mark, color/white horizontal logos, favicon, Apple touch icon, and web icons into the app's `src/assets/` and `public/` locations. Vite includes the imported milestone art in the application build.

After Capacitor generates `android/`, `python scripts/generate-icons.py --android` copies the supplied density-specific launcher resources into `android/app/src/main/res/`. `python scripts/android-post-setup.py <versionCode> <versionName>` installs the supplied white splash mark. The Android project remains generated by CI and is not committed.

The 512×512 Play icon and 1024×500 feature graphic remain in the source kit for Play Console upload; they are not included in the APK as listing media. Choose logo color variants according to the asset guide: color on light backgrounds, white on dark/gradient backgrounds, and mono for one-color output. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects.
